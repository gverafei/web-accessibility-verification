Africa

The Harvard community’s impact in Africa can be seen in the work of the Africa Research Center, the Harvard Malaria Initiative, the Harvard University Center for African Studies, and the Motsepe Presidential Research Accelerator Fund for Africa.

Delivering care in Madagascar

Researchers from Harvard Medical School and the Ministry of Public Health of Madagascar created a model health system that provides universal access to basic health care to the region’s 200,000 residents.

Delivering care in Madagascar

Designing solutions in Mozambique

Design Engineering graduates from Harvard traveled to Mozambique to explore one of the most effective drought response systems.

Designing solutions in Mozambique

Fostering growth in Africa's economy

Research from Harvard Business School shows how fintech firms could help create more than a million jobs in Africa.

Fostering growth in Africa's economy

Creating healthcare infrastructure across Africa

Harvard Kennedy School alum Akonkwa Mubagwa’s venture, Winko Solar, is building solar energy systems that power healthcare infrastructure across the continent.

Creating healthcare infrastructure across Africa

Helping coastal Africa with climate changes

Harvard’s Salata Institute examined what interventions remain possible as coastal populations face a rapidly advancing sea.

Fostering global education

Harvard’s Global Education Innovation Initiative is a multi-country research-policy-practice collaborative examining effective education in the 21st century for all students, including marginalized youth.

Asia

From the cross-cultural studies at Harvard’s Asia Center and the Lakshmi Mittal and Family South Asia Institute to the comprehensive East Asian collections of the Harvard Yenching Library and research from the Middle East Initiative, the Harvard community has a wealth of resources to help scholars explore and celebrate Asia.

Middle East Dialogues

Harvard Professor Tarek Masoud created the Middle East Dialogues series to have incisive conversations with guests representing very different perspectives on the Israel-Gaza crisis, and to provide students with opportunities to see difficult but civil conversations in action.

Middle East Dialogues

Helping Syria navigate change

Europe

Many centers and programs across Harvard, including the Europe Research Center in Paris, the Minda de Gunzburg Center for European Studies, and the Center for Hellenic Studies, provide academic resources and opportunities for students in Cambridge and Europe.

Rural Europe needs a spotlight

A study shows that rural residents of the European Union feel invisible to the very policymakers charged with representing their interests. Harvard Sociologist Michèle Lamont developed the concept of recognition gaps to help. “It’s not only about distribution of resources,” she says. “It’s also about how people create their identity, how they make sense of their place in the world.”
