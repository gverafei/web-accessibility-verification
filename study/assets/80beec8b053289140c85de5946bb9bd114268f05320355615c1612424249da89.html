ROSE Data Server (RDS)

RDS is a high-performance, all-in-one storage, 100G networking, and container platform designed for enterprise environments. It features 20 U.2 NVMe storage slots and a special RouterOS Edition for Storage & Compute (ROSE).

  • 20 × U.2 NVMe storage slots
  • 100G high-speed networking
  • 16-core 2 GHz ARM CPU
  • 32 GB DDR4 RAM
  • ROSE (RouterOS Edition)
  • Container-ready
  • Dual hot-swappable power supplies
