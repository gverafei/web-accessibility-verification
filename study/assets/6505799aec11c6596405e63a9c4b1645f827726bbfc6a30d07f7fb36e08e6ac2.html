The NAI Comments on Vermont Age-Appropriate Design Code Proposed Rules

On October 2, the NAI submitted comments to Vermont Attorney General Charity Clark on proposed rules implementing the Vermont Age-Appropriate Design Code. The NAI supports the law’s goal of protecting minors, while raising concerns about the draft rules’ blanket exclusion of advertising.

Read more →

The NAI Comments on FTC Proposed Enforcement Policy Statement on Personalized Pricing

The NAI agrees that covertly raising prices based on consumer data can violate Section 5 of the FTC Act, and urges the Commission to distinguish this from data-driven discounts such as loyalty programs.

Read more →

NAI Urges Governor Newsom to Veto AB 1542

The NAI urged a veto of AB 1542, warning that a ban on selling or sharing sensitive personal information—even with consumer consent—could restrict beneficial uses. The NAI calls instead for an opt-in consent standard.

Read more →
