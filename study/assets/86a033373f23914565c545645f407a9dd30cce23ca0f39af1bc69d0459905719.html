Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase a domain name?

When you start working on your website or online store, the first thing to do is to choose and register a domain name. A domain name consists of the text you choose plus a domain ending (.net, .fr, .org, etc.) registered for a period between 1 and 9 years, and renewable indefinitely.

Check availability with our search tool. If the name is available, add it to your cart and register it.

Who does a domain name belong to?

Registries manage particular domain endings (.net, .fr, .com, .org, etc.) and sell domain names under them. Registrars like Gandi are accredited by each registry to sell domain names to end users. Once registered, a domain name is associated with an Owner contact, who is its holder for a set length of time.

If a name shows as unavailable in the results of a search, someone else has registered it. You can check the owner with our whois service or look for alternatives with our suggestion tool.

How do I recover or re-purchase a domain name?

If your desired name is taken, you can find the current owner using the Whois and make an offer. The Whois is the directory of technical and contact information for domain names. It shows the registrar, registration date, expiration date, and last updated date.

The Whois is not always up to date, and GDPR prevents registrars from publishing full contact information for private individuals without consent. You can contact the organization listed, or as a last resort use the contact page of the website associated with the domain.

What is a web host?

A web host (or hosting provider) is a service provider that makes data storage and web servers available to website owners. The stored elements are what internet users see when they visit the site.

The host installs, secures, and keeps web servers up to date, and develops the underlying infrastructure. A web host is not involved in the content stored on the servers or in developing the specific websites it hosts.

Which web host should I choose?

Your website needs to be stored on a web hosting server, so choosing a provider and package that fits your needs is crucial. The risk is choosing a solution that is the wrong size, too expensive, or poorly suited to your skill level.

Whether you need a hosting solution for a blog, a hosting solution for your business activities, or hosting for developing a mobile app, there is a web hosting solution for you at Gandi.
