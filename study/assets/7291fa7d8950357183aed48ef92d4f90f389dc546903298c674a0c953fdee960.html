Frequently asked questions

Is the Brave Browser safe?

Brave blocks privacy-invasive ads and trackers, third-party data storage, and helps protect against browser fingerprinting. It upgrades supported webpages to secure HTTPS connections. Learn more about Brave privacy features.

How do I download and install Brave?

Brave is available for desktop computers and mobile devices. Get Brave for desktop, Brave for Android, or Brave for iOS.

Does Brave have a VPN?

Yes. Brave Firewall + VPN protects your connections across your entire device, including outside the Brave Browser. One subscription covers up to five devices across Android, iOS, and desktop.

Does Brave block YouTube ads?

Brave can block ads on video sites like YouTube, including on-page, pre-roll, and mid-roll ads. Learn more about Brave’s YouTube ad blocking.

What is Brave Search?

Brave Search is a private, independent search engine with its own index of the Web. It does not profile users. Learn more about Brave Search or use it in any browser.

Is Brave free?

Yes, Brave is free to use. Brave also offers subscription-based services including Brave Talk Premium and Brave Firewall + VPN.
