FAQs

What does Fastly actually do?

Fastly is an edge cloud platform that helps companies deliver fast, secure, and reliable digital experiences. It combines CDN, security (including WAF and DDoS protection), and edge computing so teams can control how content and applications are delivered and protected in real-time.

What products does Fastly offer?

Fastly offers a suite of products across performance and security, including CDN for content delivery, WAF for application protection, DDoS mitigation, bot management, and edge compute solutions for running logic closer to users. These products work together as a unified platform.

How is Fastly different from other CDNs?

Fastly is designed for real-time control and programmability at the edge. Unlike traditional CDNs, it allows developers to instantly update configurations, run custom logic, and gain deep visibility into traffic.

Who should use Fastly?

Fastly is used by developers, security teams, and digital businesses that need high performance, strong security, and flexibility. It’s especially well-suited for ecommerce, media, SaaS platforms, and companies running modern, API-driven applications.

Can Fastly replace multiple tools?

Yes. Fastly consolidates several layers of infrastructure, including CDN, WAF, DDoS protection, and edge logic, into a single platform. This helps reduce complexity, improve performance, and give teams more control over their delivery and security stack.
