About ibiblio

Home to one of the largest “collections of collections” on the Internet, ibiblio.org is an online public library with freely available software and information for topics including music, literature, art, history, science, politics, and cultural studies.

With millions of files and worldwide transactions, we deliver public access to knowledge for audiences of all interests and backgrounds.
