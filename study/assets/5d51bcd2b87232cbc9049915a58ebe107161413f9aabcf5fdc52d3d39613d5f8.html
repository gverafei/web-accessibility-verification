Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is the Brave Browser safe?

Brave blocks privacy-invasive ads and trackers, third-party data storage, and browser fingerprinting. It upgrades webpages where possible to secure HTTPS connections, and these protections are on by default. Brave is built on the open-source Chromium Web core. Learn more about privacy features.

How do I download and install Brave?

Brave is available for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS. Get the browser for desktop, for Android, or for iOS.

Does Brave have a VPN?

Yes. Brave Firewall + VPN protects everything you do online on your entire device, even outside the Brave Browser. One subscription covers up to five devices across Android, iOS, and desktop.

Does Brave block YouTube ads?

Brave can block ads on video sites like YouTube, including on-page, pre-roll, and mid-roll ads. Learn more about Brave’s YouTube ad blocking.

What is the Brave Search engine?

Brave Search is a private, independent search engine with its own index of the Web and AI-answer features. It does not profile users. You can use it in any browser at search.brave.com. Learn more about Brave Search.

What is Brave Origin?

Brave Origin is a paid version of the browser for users who want Brave’s privacy, ad blocking, and speed without some additional features. Visit Brave Origin to learn more.

What languages is Brave available in?

The Brave Browser is available in nearly 160 languages, including four different dialects of Chinese. Brave Search is currently available in almost 20 languages.

Who owns Brave?

The Brave Browser, Brave Search, and their features are made by Brave Software Inc, an independent, privately held company.

Is Brave open source?

Yes. Brave is built on the open-source Chromium Web core, and its client code is released under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.

How does Brave compare to Chrome?

By blocking privacy-invading ads and trackers by default, Brave has less to load on each webpage. Learn more about Brave and Chrome.

Is Brave free?

Yes, Brave is free to use. Download Brave for desktop, for Android, or for iOS. Subscription features include Brave Talk Premium and Brave Firewall + VPN.

What is BAT, and how do I earn it?

BAT is short for Basic Attention Token and is an optional part of Brave Rewards. Users may choose to view privacy-protecting ads and earn BAT through the Brave Rewards program. Brave Wallet offers a way to store BAT and other assets.
