About Us

Founded in 2018, TripLink is a wholly owned subsidiary of Trip.com Group. Our footprint has already spread to Hong Kong, Shanghai, Singapore, London, Amsterdam, Tokyo, Seoul, Bangkok, Jakarta and Kuala Lumpur.

Guided by our mission, "Technology Empowers Finance", TripLink is dedicated to building a secure and efficient global payment network that connects directly to local settlement systems. This network is designed to address diverse requirements, including cross-border payments for e-commerce, general trade, and international travel.

At TripLink, we are devoted to being a trusted partner, forging a prosperous future in partnership with our valued clients.
