Public Policy

  • The NAI Comments on Vermont Age-Appropriate Design Code Proposed Rules

    On October 2, the NAI submitted comments to Vermont Attorney General Charity Clark on proposed rules implementing the Vermont Age-Appropriate Design Code (VTAADC). The NAI supports the law’s goal of protecting minors from harmful data practices and manipulative design, but cautions that the draft rules’ blanket exclusion of all advertising would eliminate even low-risk practices.

    Read more about the Vermont comments

  • The NAI Comments on FTC Proposed Enforcement Policy Statement on Personalized Pricing

    On September 25, the NAI submitted comments to the Federal Trade Commission on its Proposed Enforcement Policy Statement Regarding Personalized Pricing. The NAI agrees that covertly raising prices based on consumer data can violate Section 5 of the FTC Act, but urges the Commission to make clear that data-driven discounts, such as loyalty programs, are distinct.

    Read more about the FTC comments

  • NAI Urges Governor Newsom to Veto AB 1542, Calling for Opt-In Consent Standard Instead

    On September 9, the NAI sent a letter to California Governor Gavin Newsom urging a veto of AB 1542, warning that its outright ban on selling or sharing sensitive personal information, even with consumer consent, would restrict beneficial uses like health advertising and location-based services.

    Read more about AB 1542
