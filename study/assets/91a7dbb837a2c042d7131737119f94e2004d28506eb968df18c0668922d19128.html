FAQs

Is the Brave Browser safe?

Brave is one of the safest browsers on the market today. It blocks privacy-invasive ads and trackers, blocks third-party data storage, protects from browser fingerprinting, upgrades webpages to secure https connections where possible, and does all this by default.

It’s built off the open-source Chromium Web core. Learn more.

How do I download and install Brave?

Brave is available on desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) and on Android and iOS. Download the Brave browser for desktop, for Android (opens in a new tab), or for iOS (opens in a new tab).

Does Brave have a VPN?

Yes. Brave Firewall + VPN protects everything you do online, on your entire device, even outside the Brave Browser. One subscription covers up to 5 devices, across Android, iOS, and desktop.

Does Brave block YouTube ads?

Yes, Brave can block ads on video sites like YouTube, including on-page, pre-roll, mid-roll, and end-card ads. Learn more about Brave’s YouTube ad blocking.

What is the Brave Search engine?

Brave Search is a private, independent search engine and the default for most new Brave users. It is also available in any other browser (opens in a new tab) at search.brave.com. Learn more about Brave Search.

What is Brave Origin, and how can I get started using it?

Brave Origin is a paid version of the browser for users who don’t need every feature Brave offers but still want its privacy protections. Visit brave.com/origin to learn more and get started.

What languages is Brave available in?

The Brave Browser is available in nearly 160 languages, including four different dialects of Chinese. Brave Search is currently available in almost 20 languages.

Who owns Brave?

The Brave Browser, Brave Search, and their features are made by Brave Software Inc, an independent, privately-held company.

Is Brave open source?

Yes. The Brave Browser is built on the open-source Chromium Web core, and our client code is released under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (opens in a new tab).

Is Brave free?

Yes, Brave is completely free to use. Brave also offers subscription features, including Brave Talk Premium and Brave Firewall + VPN.
