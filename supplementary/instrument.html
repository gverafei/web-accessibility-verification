Human-review instrument

What to review

  1. Identify the target as Acquired, Patched or Regenerated.
  2. Apply its category contract below.
  3. Inspect all reference and target views, including the lower page. Compare corresponding content rather than identical coordinates.
  4. Check that main information and meaningful images are coherent and legible, and that important content and controls are not obscured by overlap or clipping.
  5. Check that essential visible links, buttons and form fields are identifiable and their purpose understandable.
  6. Allow advertising changes, coherent carousel states and auxiliary embedded-media differences when they do not obscure or degrade the main interface. Distinguish an actual page control from a control depicted inside an illustration or demonstration.
  7. Consult the saved component context when interpreting dynamic regions. Judge the recorded evidence; testing remote interactions is not required.

Category contracts

Acquired: the reproduction preserves the main reference information, organization and essential visible controls and remains meaningful and usable. Pre-existing accessibility violations alone do not justify rejection.

Patched: preserve main source content, images and essential controls, allowing changes necessary to remove accessibility barriers.

Regenerated: a different design, wording, image selection and control arrangement are permitted. Target information and controls must be coherent and usable for the reference purpose.

Response

Accepted: the target meets the applicable category checks.

Not accepted: the target is blank or blocked, its main information is incoherent or unreadable, or essential visible controls cannot be understood. Acquired and patched pages additionally fail when required source information or controls are lost or distorted.

Each reviewer supplies one final page judgment. The review form conceals model outcomes, model annotations, generator identity, audit scores and the other reviewer's response.
